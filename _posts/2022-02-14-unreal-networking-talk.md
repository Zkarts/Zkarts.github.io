As I had brushed up my Unreal Networking knowledge in response to working on State of Decay 2 and noticing there was a knowledge gap within my team, I decided to share my newfound knowledge within work, for anyone who would be interested in joining. The result was the attached presentation.

### Knowledge is Power
My initial driving force was the lack of Unreal Networking knowledge that I saw, but as I started to get a good grasp of the intricacies involved, I wanted to share what I learned in a brief, accessible session, sharing resources for further investigation and learning, while breaking down complex concepts such as ownership and authority in a comprehensive way. The point was to share the knowledge and share the power to make engineering decisions based on the existing system we build upon, enabling more and more of us, including designers, to work on a multiplayer game with the required knowledge and backed up decisions.
